✦ Skywheel Privacy Policy

Effective November 7, 2026 · Applies to the Skywheel apps for iPhone, iPad and Android, and the Skywheel web version.

The short version

Skywheel works on your device. Birth details, notes and journal entries stay on your phone, tablet or computer unless you choose to export or share them. Skywheel has no accounts, no ads, no tracking and no analytics, and it does not sell or share your data with anyone.

What Skywheel stores, and where

  • Birth details you enter (names, dates, times and places of birth), your settings, notes and daily check-ins are stored only on your device.
  • If you turn on the PIN lock, this data is encrypted on your device with AES-256 using a key made from your PIN. Your PIN itself is never stored or sent anywhere.
  • The home-screen widget reads a small summary (Moon phase, Moon sign, the day's aspects and your top transit) that the app saves on the same device.
  • Your device's own backup (iCloud Backup or Google backup) may include Skywheel's data, under Apple's or Google's privacy terms. You control this in your device settings.

What Skywheel sends off your device

  • Nothing automatically. Charts, forecasts and readings are calculated on your device.
  • Online place search (optional). If a birthplace is not in the built-in list and you tap Search online for another place, the place name you typed is sent to the Open-Meteo geocoding service (open-meteo.com) to look up its coordinates and time zone. No birth date, time, name or other details are sent with it, and nothing is sent unless you tap that button.
  • When you choose to export a backup, share a picture or share a friend code, the file or text goes wherever you send it (for example iCloud Drive, Google Drive or a message). Those services handle it under their own privacy terms.
  • A friend code contains the birth details of the person who shared it. Only share yours with people you trust.

Notifications

If you turn on alerts, Skywheel schedules them on your device. No push-notification server is used, and you can turn alerts off at any time in the app or in your device settings.

Children

Skywheel is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Deleting your data

Delete people inside the app, use Settings → “Delete everything”, or uninstall Skywheel to remove everything it stored on your device. Backup files you exported yourself must be deleted from wherever you saved them.
